---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-10-12
  Changes:
    - With fse-std 0.7+, the editor's layout as our other mods': Add key action / command / loadout / bundle on a
      tools rail out of its right edge, Edit, Remove, Up, Down and, apart from them, Import, Export and Preview on a
      dock below it, the look and feel (the per-player mod settings) behind a gear by the close button, with Docked
      first (unticked: all inside), Theme... and Reset size and place (fse-std's shared texts). It moves, resizes and
      remembers its place. Without fse-std it looks as before. The optional fse-std dependency is now 0.7.0.
    - The settings panel: checkboxes before their text, as in our other mods. Every setting has a description.
    - Esc closes the editor; Preview and the radial key open the radial over it without closing it, and Esc goes back
      to the editor after. Docked or Reset from the gear's panel no longer leaves Esc pointing at the old window.
      Closing the editor with its X also closes an open "Remove?" question.
    - Import, Export and Preview are greyed out while an entry's form is open.
    - Form headings in sentence case ("New key action"); the type after a title only when the title doesn't already
      say it ("Loadout", not "Loadout  Loadout"); entries without an icon show their kind's.
    - Shortcut icons in the editor's lists show on dark and light themes (the game's black icons made white on a dark
      window, the white ones of coloured shortcuts black on a light one).
    - A key action saved without a title takes the game's name for what it fires (e.g. "Undo"), not its internal one.
    - The ring's icons are bigger: Icon size 40 by default (28 before; a 28 left from before becomes 40).
    - Remove and Delete ask first (a bundle goes with everything in it); without fse-std, a second click removes.
    - Edit, Remove, Up and Down are greyed out while nothing is selected (Up / Down also at the ends).
    - The filter has a search icon; the breadcrumb starts at "All actions".
    - Entries without an icon line up with the rest.
    - Edit forms scroll, with Cancel and Save always in sight.
